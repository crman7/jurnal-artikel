About Apple Inc.

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The company's products include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home, and Accessories (Apple Watch, AirPods, Apple TV). Services include the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, iCloud, and AppleCare.

The Bullish Thesis (Potensi Naik)

Driver pertumbuhan masa depan
  • ✔ Ekosistem Apple Intelligence (AI): Siklus upgrade iPhone besar-besaran yang didorong fitur AI generatif on-device terbaru.
  • ✔ Pendapatan Services Berulang (High Margin): Pendapatan dari App Store, Cloud, dan Pay tumbuh konsisten dengan margin kotor di atas 70%.

The Bearish Thesis (Faktor Risiko)

Tantangan valuasi & regulasi
